About

A small Swiss company, one product

We build the thing we needed and could not buy: something that files the documents accounting software ignores, and asks before it moves them.

Why it exists

Receipts are solved. Everything else is not.

Swiss accounting software handles receipts well. Scan, book, done. Nobody has to remember where anything went.

The documents that commit the company to something get worse treatment. A contract is signed, scanned, and lands in a folder named by whoever happened to save it. A policy renews. Minutes are written and never read again. The information is there. Retrieving it depends on memory.

That gap is the whole product. Not a document management system, a filing assistant that reads, proposes, and waits.

Principles

Four rules we do not trade away.

Nothing moves without approval

A person decides, every time. We will not add a mode that files silently, however often it is asked for.

Your documents stay yours

Read where they sit, never copied out, never used to train anything. We hold nothing you would have to ask for back.

One product

Not a local version and a better cloud version. The same software, wherever your files live.

Nothing locked in

Your documents keep working without us: normal files, normal folders, readable names. Leaving costs you search, not access.

Who you speak to

No support tier in between.

Built in Switzerland. Where hosting applies, it is Swiss. The person who installs your machine is the person who answers when you write, and they can change the code.

We stay small enough for that to remain true. If it stops being true, something has gone wrong with the plan.
