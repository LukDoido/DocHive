About the product

Can it read scans and handwriting?+

Printed text is reliable, scans included. Handwriting is not. Signatures and short notes are usually read. A form filled in by hand often is not. Those documents are flagged for a person instead of guessed at.

What if it files something wrong?+

You correct it in the queue before anything moves. If a wrong proposal was approved, one click puts the file back under its original name in its original folder, and the log shows who did what. See how the review queue works.

Can it work with our existing folder structure?+

It is the only way it works. DocHive files into folders that exist and follows the pattern you already use. No new structure, no parallel archive.

Which languages does it handle?+

German, French, Italian and English, Swiss spelling included. A German contract with English annexes is read as one document.

What happens if the internet drops?+

On premise, everything keeps running: reading, filing, search, deadlines. Only email reminders wait for the line to come back. On SharePoint or Google Drive, DocHive waits for your cloud like everything else does.

Do we need IT staff?+

No. We install and configure DocHive. Day to day, someone approves proposals in a browser. If you have an IT partner, we speak to them about the network and leave you out of it.

About your data

The section to forward. Also covered on what leaves the building.

Where do our documents actually go?+

Nowhere. They stay in the folder or drive they are in now. On premise they are read locally, in your office. On SharePoint or Google Drive they are read through the official API and stay in your tenant.

Who else can see our documents?+

Nobody outside your company. We hold no copy and have no standing access. If you ask us to look at a problem, someone grants access for that session and it ends with the session.

Is our data used to train anything?+

No. Not ours, not anyone else's. Your corrections stay local and improve proposals for you only.

What exactly leaves our network?+

One licence check a day, carrying a key and the software version. No filenames, no content. The exception is optional cloud processing for difficult documents: off by default, and when on, only the documents you send to it leave.

Is there an audit log, and who can read it?+

Yes. Every proposal, edit, approval, rejection and undo, with user, timestamp, old name and new name. You decide who can read it. It exports to CSV, which is usually what an auditor wants.

What happens to our documents if we stop paying?+

Nothing happens to them. They are your files, in your folders, under the names they were filed with. You lose search, reminders and new proposals. There is no export to run, because there is nothing of yours in here to release.

How does it handle access rights and confidential HR files?+

It inherits the rights already on your folders. If only HR can open a folder, only HR sees those documents in search, and only the people you nominate review HR proposals. DocHive does not widen access to anything.

What about backups?+

DocHive is not a backup and does not replace one. Your documents stay in the system you already back up. DocHive holds the search index and the log locally. Both can be rebuilt.

About buying

Prices and day rates are on the pricing page.

What does setup involve and how long does it take?+

One day on site for most companies: set up DocHive, connect the folders and mailboxes, write down your naming rules, show the reviewers. Rules are then tuned over the first few weeks, when the first awkward documents turn up.

Do you come to us?+

Yes, on every plan. We would rather see how you file than be told about it.

What if we have several legal entities?+

One installation can serve several, with separate folders, rules and rights. Each additional entity is CHF 1,200 a year.

What happens if we cancel?+

First term is 12 months, then year to year with three months' notice to the end of the term. The appliance is yours to keep. Filed documents stay filed.

Can we buy it outright instead of subscribing?+

Yes. A perpetual licence with annual maintenance. Ask on the call and we will price both, so you can compare.
