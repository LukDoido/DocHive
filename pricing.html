Setup

Setup is on site, on every plan.

Someone comes to you, connects the folders and mailboxes you already use, and writes your naming rules into the system while watching how you actually file.

It is a one time project, quoted after the call. We would rather see your folders than guess at them.

Billing questions

Four we get every time.

Is the price per user?+

No. Per company. Everyone who needs access has it. The plan follows your headcount band, not your seats.

What does setup cost?+

Quoted after the call, once we have seen your folders. It is a one time project and separate from the licence, we will not put a figure on this page that we would have to revise in your office.

Can we pay monthly?+

Yes, by direct debit or card. Annual billing is ten instalments instead of twelve, so the year costs two months less.

What happens after the first year?+

It continues year to year. Three months' notice to the end of the term. Your documents stay in your folders under their filed names, whatever you decide, see what happens if you stop paying.
